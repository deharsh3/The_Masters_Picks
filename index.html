Team Selection Instructions

How to Submit Your Golf Pool Picks

Select 6 golfers total using the selection menus:

  • 2 Favorites — Top 20 world ranked players in the tournament field
  • 2 Mid-Tier players — Next 20 world ranked players
  • 2 Longshots — All remaining players in the field

Enter your name and submit your team once all six players are selected.

You may change your picks at any time before the tournament begins. Simply submit a new team using the exact same name. Your newest submission will overwrite your previous entry.

You can view all submitted teams on the View Teams page to confirm your picks or make sure your changes were saved correctly.

Understanding the Odds

Players are grouped into tiers based on their Official World Golf Ranking. The top 20 ranked players in the tournament field are placed in the Favorites tier, the next 20 players are placed in the Mid-Tier, and all remaining players are classified as Longshots.

Each player is also assigned an estimated betting odds value based on their world ranking. These odds are used only for tiebreaking purposes.

For example, a player with odds of 4.5 is considered a strong favorite. This means that for every $1 wagered, the payout would be $4.50 if the player wins. Players with higher numbers such as 60 or 100 are much less likely to win according to sportsbooks.

Odds are used only as a tiebreaker in this pool. If two teams finish with the same score, the winner will be the team whose five lowest-odds players have the highest average odds. This rewards teams that take slightly bigger risks rather than selecting only the strongest favorites.

Tournament Scoring

Each team's score is calculated using the best 4 scores out of your 6 golfers.

If teams are tied, the tiebreaker will be determined by the highest average betting odds of the 5 lowest-odds players on the roster. This prevents teams from gaining an advantage by selecting extremely longshot players.

Picks will lock when the tournament begins.
